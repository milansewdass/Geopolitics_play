Strategy of nations

GEOPOLITICS

Take command of one nation and face nine rivals across a real world map. Soldiers come from population, battles from dice, and every move your opponents make is shown and explained. Own all ten countries to win.

New Game Continue Game Full manual
How to play
1 · CHOOSE

Pick your nation. Nine more are drawn at random; each starts with soldiers set by its population.

2 · REINFORCE

From round 2 every country you hold earns 2 new soldiers at the start of your turn.

3 · ATTACK

Once per turn, strike any enemy from a country with 2+ soldiers. Matched dice decide each phase.

4 · CONQUER

Hold all ten countries to win. Anyone can watch a campaign through its share link.
